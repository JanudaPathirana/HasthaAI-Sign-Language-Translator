🧠 How It Works

Our AI system follows 3 simple steps:

1

👁 Perceive

The camera detects the hand and finds 21 joint points in 3D space using MediaPipe Hands.

2

🤔 Think

The AI model compares the joint positions with trained gesture patterns and gives a result.

3

⚡ Act

The matched gesture is translated to English and Sinhala, then spoken aloud.
